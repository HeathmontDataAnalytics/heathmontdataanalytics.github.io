When developing your research question, check that suitable data is available to answer it. Some good places to look:

- [Gapminder](https://www.gapminder.org/data/)
- [Maven Analytics Data Playground](https://mavenanalytics.io/data-playground)
- [Australian Bureau of Statistics (ABS)](https://www.abs.gov.au/)
- [data.gov.au](https://data.gov.au/)
